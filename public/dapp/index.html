Sovereign Trust Token (SIT)

Self-issued, private/permissioned stablecoin backed by Treasury, Core Banking, Trust Accounting, Bond/Fixed Income, Cash, Sub-Ledger and CRM reserves. Gasless transfers via ERC-2771 trusted forwarder.

Mint SIT (Fiat / Reserve On-Ramp)

Debit a source-of-funds ledger and mint SIT to a wallet. This creates the on-chain token and credits the reserve account.

Burn SIT (Fiat / Reserve Off-Ramp)

Burn SIT from a wallet and credit the source-of-funds ledger. The operator must be approved as a burner or tokens must be sent to the operator first.

Gasless SIT Transfer (Meta-Tx)

Sign with MetaMask; the operator relayer pays gas. No ETH required in the sender wallet.

Gasless SIT Transfer (EIP-4337 Account Abstraction)

Smart-account wallet + verifying paymaster. User holds zero native ETH; the operator paymaster funds gas. SIT tokens are the only asset the user needs.
