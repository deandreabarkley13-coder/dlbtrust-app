Privacy Policy

Last updated 30 August 2026

This policy explains how the DeAndrea LaVar Barkley Trust (the “Trust”), a non-depository private family trust company organized under Ohio law, handles personal information in connection with this website and the trust administration platform operated at this domain (together, the “Platform”).

Who the Platform is for

The Platform is a private back-office system. It is used by the Trust’s trustees and administrators, by beneficiaries of the Trust, and by vendors and counterparties the Trust pays. It is not a consumer financial product, it is not offered to the general public, and it does not accept deposits.

Information we collect

  • Identity and contact details — name, email address and any message you send through the request or contact forms on this site.
  • Beneficiary and vendor records — distribution requests, payment instructions, invoices and the account or wallet details needed to pay you.
  • Transaction records — ledger entries, approvals, payment references and settlement confirmations arising from trust administration.
  • Technical data — authentication sessions, security logs and audit records of actions taken in the Platform.

Why we use it

To administer the Trust: to evaluate and record distribution requests, to pay obligations the Trust owes, to maintain the double-entry books and trust accounting records the Trust is required to keep, to meet tax and regulatory reporting duties, and to secure the Platform against unauthorized access.

Who we share it with

We do not sell personal information and we do not use it for advertising. We disclose it only to service providers who carry out these functions for the Trust — hosting, banking, payment processing, digital-asset settlement, accounting and professional advisers — and only to the extent each needs it. Where a payment is made, the information necessary to complete that payment is shared with the provider settling it. We also disclose information where law, subpoena or regulatory obligation requires it.

Payment and digital-asset processing

Payments made by or to the Trust may be settled through third-party providers, including banking partners, payment processors and regulated digital-asset on-ramps. Those providers collect and process information under their own privacy policies and their own identity-verification obligations; this policy does not govern their handling of it. Transactions settled on a public blockchain are recorded on that blockchain permanently and publicly, and cannot be deleted or altered by the Trust.

Retention

Trust records — ledger entries, approvals, distributions and supporting documents — are retained for as long as the Trust exists and thereafter for the period required by law and by the Trust’s own recordkeeping obligations. Audit logs are append-only and are not edited.

Security

Access to the Platform requires authentication and is limited by role. Sensitive operations require approval by a second trustee. Credentials and keys are held in secret storage and are not exposed in the application. No system is perfectly secure, and we do not represent otherwise.

Your choices

You may ask us what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, and ask us to delete information we are not required to keep. Requests can be made through the contact form. Records the Trust must retain for legal, tax or fiduciary reasons, and data already written to a public blockchain, cannot be deleted.

Children

Where a beneficiary is a minor, information about them is provided and managed by their parent, guardian or the trustees. The Platform is not directed to children generally.

Changes

We may update this policy. The revision date at the top of this page reflects the current version.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the trustees through the contact form on this site.
