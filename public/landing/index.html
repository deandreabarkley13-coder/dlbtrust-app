About the Barkley Family Trust

The Barkley Family Private Trust Company is organized under Ohio law as a non-depository family trust company. It serves as custodian and issuer of family support obligations, stewarding principal and fixed-income reserves for the benefit of current and future generations. We do not accept deposits from the public, act as a bank, or lend against trust assets. All distributions follow the trust instrument, fiduciary principles, and maker/checker approval protocols.

Family Wealth Learning

A private trust company is a purpose-built vehicle for families who want professional custody, transparent distributions, and a clear ledger of principal, income, and support. These primers explain how the Barkley Family Trust manages capital while preserving privacy.

1

What Is a Private Trust Company?

A non-depository fiduciary entity that administers family assets, performs custodial functions, and issues support under state trust law.

2

Principal vs. Coupon Income

Principal remains in bond and fixed-income reserves; coupon income is accrued and made available for beneficiary support.

3

Maker / Checker Control

No single person can move trust funds. Distributions require two-trustee review and a clear audit trail before execution.

4

Reserve Backing

All support credits are backed by the trust ledger, bond portfolio, and fixed-income engines before any payout is sent.

5

Beneficiary Portal

Approved beneficiaries can view balances, request support, and track distributions without accessing trustee controls.

6

Tax & Reporting

Trust accounting, income allocation, and tax engine data flow into a single source of truth for trustees and professionals.

Beneficiary Distribution Request

Submit a request for a trust distribution or disbursement. The system will create a secure record, run an asset-to-debt proof, and send one-time approval notifications to the maker and checker trustees.
